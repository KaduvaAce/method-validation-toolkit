\## Notes

\- LOD uses a factor of 3, not 3.3. LOD/LOQ ratio = 3.33, internally consistent.

\- Methods state five concentrations from 7.813 to 125 ug/mL; Table 1 states

&#x20; N = 8 and a range of 0.122-125 ug/mL.

\- Reported LOD is far below the lowest calibration standard, so it is an

&#x20; extrapolation beyond the validated range.

\- Intra-day %RSD values of 0.022-0.128% are far below typical HPLC injection

&#x20; precision; possible scaling error.
